WEATHER OUTLOOK

Nine forecast sources for anywhere on Earth, averaged into one number — and the spread between them, so you can see when they disagree.

    Pick a day and the hours that matter. Every source gets read against that window instead of the whole day, and the day gets a verdict.

    Search for a place to begin.

    How this number is made

    Sources are not equally skilful, so they are not weighted equally: at a week or more out the ECMWF ensemble is the best estimate here and a single deterministic run is close to noise. The bar fills to the weighted average; the faint band behind it spans the lowest and highest figure any one source gives, so a short band means they agree.
